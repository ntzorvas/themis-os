# EU Regulations Referenced in AIoD OC1

## 1. Digital Europe Programme Regulation
- **Regulation (EU) 2021/694** of 29 April 2021 (Digital Europe Programme)
- Budget: €7.5 billion (2021–2027)
- Art. 12(6): Restricts open calls to entities established in/controlled by EU Member States (+ eligible associated countries)
- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32021R0694

## 2. De Minimis Regulation (if applicable)
- **Regulation (EU) 2023/2831** of 13 December 2023
- Replaces Regulation (EU) No 1407/2013
- Threshold: €300,000 per undertaking per 3-year period (raised from €200,000)
- Scope: Applies 1 January 2024 – 31 December 2030
- Note for AIoD: The call uses EU grant funds (not state aid), so de minimis may apply at national level for related support, not directly for this EU-funded grant
- Central register requirement: from 1 January 2026, Member States must register all de minimis aid
- URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2831/oj/eng

## 3. GDPR — Data Protection
- **Regulation (EU) 2016/679** (General Data Protection Regulation)
- AIoD OC1 SGA references: F6S (Limited) acts as Data Controller for application data
- Data stored in Google Drive and Microsoft Azure
- Confidentiality: 5 years post-completion (DeployAI ends 31 December 2027 → until December 2032)
- Beneficiary data protection obligations during implementation

## 4. EU Charter of Fundamental Rights
- Referenced indirectly via ethical AI requirements
- Proposals must address ethical, privacy, responsible AI concerns

## 5. AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)
- Not explicitly cited in OC1 but implied by "trustworthy AI" and "ethical AI" requirements
- TRL 7+ solutions with EU market deployment must comply

## 6. Financial Regulation (EU) 2018/1046
- Applies to EU-funded programs generally
- Audit rights, record-keeping (5 years), conflict of interest rules referenced in SGA
