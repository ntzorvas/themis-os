# AIoD Open Call #1 — Private Sector (OC1-PrivateSector)

## Key Dates
- Applications Open: 8 April 2026
- Submission Deadline: **8 June 2026 at 17:00 CEST**
- Eligibility & Technical Check: 8/6 – 30/6/2026
- Application Evaluation: 1/7 – 31/7/2026
- Winners Announcement: up to 10 August 2026
- Programme Implementation: 1 September 2026 – 15 April 2027

## Parent Project
- DeployAI Project, Digital Europe Programme (DIGITAL)
- Grant Agreement No. 101146490 (DIGITAL-2022-CLOUD-AI-B-03)
- Budget: €28 million | Partners: 28 from 13 EU countries
- Launched: 29 February – 1 March 2024, Berlin

## Funding Structure (AI Providers)
| Stage | Period | Participants | Funding |
|-------|--------|-------------|---------|
| Stage 1: Pitch & Select | Sep 2026 (1 month) | 50 | €5,000 |
| Stage 2: Integration | Oct–Dec 2026 (3 months) | 20 | €35,000 |
| Stage 3: Deployment | Jan 15 – Apr 15 2027 (3 months) | 10 consortia | €20,000 (Provider) |
| AI Adopters (Stage 3 only) | — | 10 | €15,000 |
| **TOTAL MAX (Provider)** | | | **€60,000** |

Funding type: **100% lump sum** (no co-financing required)

## Seven Featured Challenges
1. AI-Driven Forecasting for Grid Flexibility (federated learning, energy)
2. Predictive Maintenance for Energy Infrastructure
3. Smart Healthcare Insights Generation
4. Kubernetes-Enabled Real-World AI Solutions (climate/health/logistics)
5. AI-Builder Pipeline with NER+KG (Named Entity Recognition + Knowledge Graphs)
6. Logical Reasoning and Memory Pipeline (predicate logic, explainable AI)
7. AI-Builder Database Refactoring

## Eligibility
- **Applicant types (Stage 1&2):** Single legal entity — startup, SME, mid-cap, or research org (RTO/academia)
- **Stage 3:** Consortium = AI Provider (Stage 2 winner) + AI Adopter
- **Geography:** EU Member States + DIGITAL Europe associated countries
- **TRL:** 7 or above (mandatory)
- **Containerization:** Docker or equivalent (mandatory)
- **Pre-registration:** AIoD Platform + Business Navigator (before submission)
- **Max applications:** 1 per applicant
- **Subcontracting:** NOT eligible

## What's NOT Eligible
- TRL below 7
- No ethical/privacy/responsible AI demonstration
- No clear AIoD platform alignment
- More than 1 application per entity

## Eligible Cost Categories
- Project management
- Research and technical development
- Testing and piloting
- Communication, promotion, exploitation
- Travel (as required)
- Human resources, other direct costs, indirect costs
