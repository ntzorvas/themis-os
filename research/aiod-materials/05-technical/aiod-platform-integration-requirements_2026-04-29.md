# AIoD Platform — Technical Integration Requirements

## Minimum Technical Entry Requirements
1. **TRL 7 or above** — Product must be at commercial prototype / demonstrable in real environment
2. **Containerized format** — Docker or equivalent (mandatory, non-negotiable)
3. **EU27 market readiness** — Must demonstrate ability to serve EU market
4. **Registered on AIoD Platform** before application submission
5. **Registered on Business Navigator** before application submission

## AIoD Platform Core Components
- **Marketplace (marketplace.aiodp.ai):** Catalog of AI resources, SME-facing
- **Business Navigator (business-navigator.aiodp.ai):** Strategic matching, EU opportunities
- **Research Portal (research.aiodp.eu):** Academic and R&D layer
- **DevOps tools:** Pipeline building, training, deployment
- **Open APIs:** Support interoperability across cloud/edge/HPC

## Integration Architecture
- Cloud-native deployment
- Edge computing support
- HPC (EuroHPC) connectivity via CSC connectors
- EOSC (European Open Science Cloud) interoperability
- API-first design

## Ecosystem Integrations (Platform connects to)
- AI Factories
- European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIHs)
- Testing & Experimentation Facilities for AI (TEFs)
- Data Spaces (European common data infrastructure)
- SIMPL (cloud-to-edge continuum)
- EuroHPC

## Stage 2 — Full Integration Deliverables
From the Guide for Applicants, Stage 2 requires:
- Full integration of AI Product in AIoD Platform
- Implementation of Business Plan on the Platform
- EU27 market readiness guarantee
- Beta-testing of the Platform (active feedback loop)
- Customer acquisition for the Platform
- Mentored by DeployAI Consortium partners

## Stage 3 — Deployment Deliverables
- Real-world deployment in specific AI Adopter
- Measurable KPIs demonstration
- Integration with AIoD Platform maintained
- Final use case transformation (public showcase)

## Applicable Technical Standards
- Trustworthy AI (EU AI Act compliant)
- Privacy-by-design (GDPR)
- Open-source principles preferred (vendor independence)
- European values: transparency, ethics, security

## Registration Steps (Pre-Application)
1. Register at www.f6s.com
2. Register on AIoD Platform at https://www.aiodp.ai/
3. Register on Business Navigator at https://business-navigator.aiodp.ai/
4. Submit application at https://www.f6s.com/aiod-oc1privatesector/apply
