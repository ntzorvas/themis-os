## Key Insight for Strategy
- OC1 is genuinely competitive: only 10 reach Stage 3 (final deployment with AI Adopter)
- Stage 1→2 funnel: 50 → 20 = 40% pass rate (relatively favorable if you reach Stage 1)
- Stage 2→3 funnel: 20 → 10 = 50% pass rate
- Critical bottleneck: the **initial application → Stage 1 selection** (unknown but likely 5-20%)
- **Greek companies** in EU AI calls: historically low participation, strong opportunity for differentiation
- **Demokritos (NCSRC)** is a Greek DeployAI consortium partner — potential local advantage/contact
