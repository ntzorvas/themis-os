# DeployAI Project Context

## Fundamentals
- **Full name:** Deployment of the AI-on-Demand Platform (DeployAI)
- **Grant Agreement:** No. 101146490
- **Programme:** Digital Europe Programme (DIGITAL-2022-CLOUD-AI-B-03)
- **Budget:** €28 million
- **Duration:** Feb 2024 – Dec 2027 (4 years)
- **Kickoff:** 29 Feb – 1 Mar 2024, Berlin, Germany
- **Partners:** 28 from 13 European countries

## Key Partners
- Research: Fraunhofer IAIS, Aalto University, DTU, ECMWF, Demokritos (Greece)
- Technology: IONOS, LightOn, FIWARE, CSC (Finland)
- Innovation: EIT Digital, AI Sweden
- AI Hubs across Europe

## AI-on-Demand Platform (AIoDP)
- **Launch:** Next-generation infrastructure launched June 2025
- **Website:** https://www.aiodp.ai/
- **Marketplace:** https://marketplace.aiodp.ai/
- **Business Navigator:** https://business-navigator.aiodp.ai/
- **Research Portal:** https://research.aiodp.eu/

## Platform Features
- Trustworthy AI resource catalogue + marketplace
- European LLMs, open APIs, edge/cloud/HPC interoperability
- Integration with AI Factories, EDIHs, TEFs, Data Spaces, SIMPL, EuroHPC
- Matchmaking services
- Stakeholder observatory
- Interactive landscape tool for knowledge exchange

## Open Calls Structure (FSTP — Financial Support to Third Parties)
- **OC1:** Private Sector (this call) — ongoing 2026
- **OC2:** Public Sector — forthcoming

## Technical Requirements for Integration
- Docker containerized format (mandatory)
- TRL 7+ (product must be commercially ready)
- Registration on AIoD Platform AND Business Navigator
- EU27 market readiness demonstration
- Interoperability with AIoD core components
- Beta-testing obligations

## Ecosystem Connections
- AI Factories (EU flagship AI compute infrastructure)
- European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIHs)
- Testing & Experimentation Facilities for AI (TEFs)
- GenAI4EU initiative
- European Open Science Cloud (EOSC) — via CSC connectors
- EuroHPC
