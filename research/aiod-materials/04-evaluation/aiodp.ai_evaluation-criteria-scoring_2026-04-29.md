# AIoD OC1-PrivateSector — Evaluation Framework

## Evaluation Moments
| Evaluation | Goal | Evaluators |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| No. 1 | Eligibility check + select top 50 for Stage 1 | IEC (internal) + External Experts |
| No. 2 | Select 20 for Stage 2 | IEC + relevant stakeholders |
| No. 3 | Select 10 consortia for Stage 3 | IEC + relevant stakeholders |

## Evaluation No. 1 — Scoring Criteria (Table 5)

| ID | Criterion | Description |
|----|-----------|-------------|
| C1 | Tech/Solution Concept | AI Product description, containerization readiness, interoperability, TRL; differentiation, innovation, scalability, EU27 market readiness; added value in AIoD Platform, alignment with Open Call and Platform, core components used. Must be unique and impactful. |
| C2 | Team Skills & Expertise | Capacity, skills, expertise to deliver; ability to scale concept into sustainable product/service beyond pilot. |
| C3 | Implementation Plan | How widely solution will be used; impact and direct contribution to AIOD and AI adoption across sectors/domains (private & public); quality of Business Model as win-win for AI providers and AIoD Platform. |
| C4 | Ambition & Scalability Potential | Clarity and ambition of long-term vision; scalability of AI product and business model; ability to expand to additional users/markets/domains; potential for growth through AIoD Platform. |

## Scoring Thresholds
- **Per criterion minimum:** 6 out of 10
- **Overall score minimum:** 18 out of 30
- Applications not meeting either threshold: EXCLUDED

## Score Normalization / Discrepancy Resolution
- Minimum 2 external evaluators per application
- If score difference > 2 points per criterion → evaluators discuss to agree
- If no agreement → third expert invited for assessment

## Ranking & Tie-Breaking (Priority Order)
1. Rule 1: Higher impact and market potential (C1 score)
2. Rule 2: Better implementation score (C3)
3. Rule 3: Better team skills score (C2)
4. Rule 4: Woman as lead coordinator

## Evaluation No. 2 — Selection for Stage 2
- 50% weight: Revised Technical & Business Proposals for AIoD Platform (same criteria as Eval 1)
- 50% weight: Pitching Session at Event 1 (Relevance & Quality)

## Evaluation No. 3 — Selection for Stage 3
- 40% weight: Submitted Project to deploy in AI Adopter
- 30% weight: Degree of integration with AIoD Platform (Stage 2 outcomes)
- 30% weight: Pitching Session at Event 2 (Relevance & Quality)
- Note: Extra criteria may be added — communicated at start of Stage 2

## Appeals Procedure
- Window: 3 working days from rejection letter
- Must be sent to: aiod.opencalls@f6s.com
- Focus ONLY on procedural aspects (admissibility, eligibility, procedure) — NOT merits
- Note: External expert scores will NOT be overturned by DeployAI

## Stage 1 Deliverables (Payment 1 — €5,000)
- R1.1: Updated Technical Description & Business Plan for AIoD Platform
- R1.2: Travel Report for Event 1
- R1.3: Answers to Official Survey regarding AIoD Platform

## Stage 2 Deliverables (Payment 2 — €35,000)
- R2.1: Travel Report for Event 2
- R2.2: Report on Stage 2 implementation + expected Deliverables/KPIs
- R2.3: Confirmation of beta-testing actions
- R2.4: Sub-project submission for Stage 3 (with AI Adopter)
